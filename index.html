About Us

Born in the Heart of the Sahyadris

Sahyadri Cricket Team was founded in 2023 by a group of passionate cricket lovers from Maval, Maharashtra. What started as casual weekend friendlies has grown into one of the most competitive amateur cricket clubs in the region in a remarkably short time.

Named after the majestic Sahyadri mountain range, our team embodies the strength, endurance, and spirit of those peaks. We play not just for victories, but for the love of the game, the camaraderie, and the pride of representing our community. In just two years, we've played 213 matches — and we're just getting started.
